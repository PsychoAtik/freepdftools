Your file is processed on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF you want to split.
  2. Type which pages you want in the box — for example 1-3, 5, 8-10 to grab pages 1 through 3, page 5, and pages 8 through 10.
  3. Press "Extract Pages" — a new PDF with just those pages will download automatically.

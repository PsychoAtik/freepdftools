How to resize a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF you want to shrink.
  2. Pick a compression level — Light keeps the most quality, Maximum gives the smallest file.
  3. Press "Resize PDF". Your browser will rebuild the file and download it automatically.

Note: this works by re-rendering each page as an image, so it's best for scanned documents or image-heavy PDFs. Text in the output will no longer be selectable or searchable.

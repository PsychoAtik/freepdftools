Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your files

The PDF and image tools on this site run entirely in your web browser. Files you select are processed locally on your device using JavaScript and are never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by FreePDFTools.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which tools are used and how visitors find the site. This collects general, non-identifying information such as approximate location (country/city level), device type, and pages visited. It does not have access to the contents of any file you process with our tools.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. For more detail on how Google uses data, see Google's own Privacy & Terms page.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for analytics and advertising purposes, as described above. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the contact page.
